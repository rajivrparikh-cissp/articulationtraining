# ExecutiveVoice Regulatory Compliance & Accessibility Standards

## 1. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 Level AAA)
As an executive leadership tool, the platform is engineered to meet strict WCAG 2.2 Level AAA compliance:
- **Contrast Ratios:** Minimum 7:1 contrast ratio for all standard body typography and 4.5:1 for large headers.
- **Large-Touch Target Areas:** Touch targets on mobile and web are minimum $48 \times 48$ CSS pixels.
- **Full Screen-Reader & ARIA Compatibility:** All live audio visualizers, countdown clocks, and diagnostic rubrics provide aria-live announcements for blind and visually impaired users.
- **Keyboard Navigation:** 100% operable via keyboard alone with high-visibility focus rings.

---

## 2. Privacy & Regulatory Mandates
- **GDPR & CCPA Compliance:** Full support for User Right to Erasure ("Delete My Audio & Transcripts") with cryptographically verified deletion logs.
- **Data Residency:** Multi-region deployment options (US, EU, APAC) to satisfy enterprise sovereign data mandates.
- **Biometric Data Protection (BIPA Compliance):** Voice recordings are processed solely for speech delivery feedback; no biometric voiceprint identity matching or cross-user biometric profiling is conducted.
